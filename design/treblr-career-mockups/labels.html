Release routes & label moves

NO LOCK-IN
Default · artist-owned◎

Remain independent

Your artist career starts independent. Self-fund releases, choose a distributor and keep control of your own masters and decisions.

Optional · release by release⇢

Sign a distribution deal

Choose a deal for selected releases or catalog when it suits you. Compare fees or revenue share, pitching support and reach. It does not make you an artist signed to a label.

Optional · expand into label ownershipPLAYER-OWNED

Create and run your own label

Found an imprint when ready, then scout and sign other artists, negotiate their terms, manage releases and run label finances. You can keep your own artist career independent or use distribution partnerships for eligible releases.

Open label office ↓
These are ongoing actions, not mutually exclusive career classes. Your own artist career can remain independent while the label signs and manages other artists. Distribution can be arranged for selected releases or catalog; it is separate from owning the imprint. Distribution terms and individual release rights still govern what can be partnered.
